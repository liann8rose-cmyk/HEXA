Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE DATE: NOVEMBER 2026

1. Introduction

HEXA ("we", "our", or "the Platform") is a social chat platform strictly for adults aged 18 and above. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data. By using HEXA you agree to this policy.

2. Who We Are

HEXA is operated as an independent platform. For any questions about this policy or your personal data, use the Support function in the HEXA menu inside the app.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide

  • Username — your chosen display name on HEXA.
  • Email address — used for account registration, login, and password recovery.
  • Password — stored in hashed form. We never see or store your plain-text password.
  • Full name — required at registration.
  • Home country — the country you choose yourself when you sign up.
  • Messages and photos — content you post in the public channel or send via direct messages.

3.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Presence data — when you are online, offline, or away, stored temporarily to show your status to other users.
  • IP address — processed when you connect, to determine your approximate country/region and to protect the platform against abuse. It is not used for tracking or advertising.
  • Country / region — derived from your IP address, used to display your flag and browse regional channels.
  • Session tokens — stored locally on your device to keep you logged in.
  • "Stay logged in" token — only if you choose "Yes" when HEXA asks whether to keep you logged in. A secure sign-in token (not your password) is saved on your device so you can open HEXA without typing your password again. It is removed when you press Log Out, it expires by itself after 6 months (you then sign in again), and you can decline the option at sign-in.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the HEXA platform and its features.
  • To display your username, status, and messages to other users.
  • To authenticate your identity and secure your account.
  • To send account-related emails (password recovery, verification).
  • To enforce our age restriction and community rules.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud database provider with industry-standard encryption at rest and in transit (TLS/SSL). Session tokens are stored in your browser's localStorage and sessionStorage and are transmitted only to our backend provider (Supabase) to authenticate your requests — they are never shared with anyone else.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data (username, email) is retained for as long as your account is active.
  • Accounts that have not been used for 1 year are automatically and permanently deleted, together with their messages, cards, penalties and other account data.
  • Public channel messages and direct messages are automatically and permanently deleted 24 hours after they are sent. Each message is deleted individually based on the time it was created.
  • Messages you delete manually are removed from our database immediately.
  • Presence data (online status) is temporary and purged regularly.

7. Age Restriction

HEXA is strictly for users aged 18 and above. HEXA is an open chat where adults talk with people they do not know, and the platform is built and moderated for adults only.

We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we become aware that a user is under 18, their account will be terminated immediately and their data deleted permanently.

By registering on HEXA, you confirm that you are at least 18 years of age.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

To exercise any of these rights, use the Support function in the HEXA menu inside the app.

9. Cookies and Local Storage

HEXA does not use tracking cookies. We use browser localStorage and sessionStorage solely to maintain your login session, and, if you choose "Stay logged in", to remember that choice on your device until you log out. Your password is never stored on your device. No third-party analytics or advertising cookies are used.

10. Third-Party Services

We use Supabase (supabase.com) as our backend database and authentication provider. Supabase processes data on our behalf under their own privacy policy.

Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com). When a page loads, your browser sends your IP address to Google to retrieve the font files, subject to Google's privacy policy.

When you set a password, we check it against the Have I Been Pwned "Pwned Passwords" service (api.pwnedpasswords.com) to warn you if it appears in known data breaches. This uses a privacy-preserving method: only the first five characters of a one-way hash of your password are sent, so your password itself is never revealed to them or to anyone else.

Our pages also load the Supabase software library from the jsDelivr content delivery network (cdn.jsdelivr.net). When a page loads, your browser sends your IP address to jsDelivr to retrieve this file, subject to their privacy policy.

We load country flag images from flagcdn.com. When a page shows flags, your browser sends your IP address to flagcdn.com to retrieve the images, subject to their privacy policy.

When someone shares a YouTube link in the chat, your browser loads a preview picture from YouTube's image servers (i.ytimg.com), which receives your IP address, subject to Google's privacy policy. Nothing is sent to youtube.com unless you choose to open the video.

We do not integrate with any advertising networks, social media trackers, or analytics platforms.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this document. Continued use of HEXA after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please use the Support function in the HEXA menu inside the app.
